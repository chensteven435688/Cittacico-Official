Privacy policy

What this website does with an account, an order, and the notes it keeps on your device.

Last updated November 2026

Cittàcico is a New York maison, since 2026, for fashion, objects, and fragrance. This note describes the website as it is built. It is not a claim of certification under any privacy statute.

The house has not published a registered company name, a data-protection officer, or a postal address for formal notices. Visits are received at the studio listed on Locations. For a question about your information, write to cittacico@gmail.com.

The pages

You may read the house without an account. The same site holds an online boutique: a bag, checkout, and a maison account. On the public site the boutique stays closed to visitors until the house opens it. What follows describes that boutique as it is built.

What you may give the house

An account asks for:

  • an email address
  • a password of at least eight characters
  • your name

You may also give, or leave blank:

  • a date of birth
  • a sex, or “Prefer not to say”
  • a country and region
  • a telephone number

A checkbox records whether you wish to receive house correspondence and private viewings. That choice is saved with the profile.

A saved delivery address keeps the recipient, the street, the city, the postal code, the country, and the region, and is offered again at checkout.

Checkout asks for an email, a delivery address, and the pieces in the bag. A checkbox, which you may leave clear, says you have read this policy and agree to occasional maison news by email. A guest order is recorded against the email you give. The checkout states that an account opened later with the same email will show that order.

The form on the Contact page does not send what you type. It is not connected to a service. If you want the house to read a message, write to cittacico@gmail.com.

Orders and payment

Placing an order sends the bag, the email, the delivery address, and the news preference to the house’s order service. While the house has no merchant account, no payment is taken. The payment step says so, and the house writes to the email on the order to confirm it and arrange settlement.

When the house turns payments on, checkout continues to Stripe. Card, Apple Pay, and other wallets are entered on Stripe’s own page. Those details are not typed into this site. Until that switch is made, nothing is charged here.

Prices are shown in US dollars. Checkout currently shows shipping and tax at zero. The page also says that complimentary standard delivery applies when fulfillment is live, and that tax will be calculated from the delivery address. The figures to rely on are the ones the house confirms in writing.

The browser

The house’s own scripts do not set a cookie. The site does not load an analytics tag, an advertising pixel, or a consent service.

The browser does keep a few notes of its own:

  • The bag, so it remains if you leave and return. When you are signed in, the same bag is saved with the account. Signing out clears the bag on that device.
  • The sign-in session, so you remain signed in. It is kept in the browser’s local storage by the sign-in library, and it refreshes while you use the site. A link in a confirmation or password email can bring you back to the account page. The password itself is not stored in the page.
  • For the life of the tab: whether you have already set aside the invitation to join the house, and, after an order, a short summary so the confirmation page can show it.

Services the pages call

The catalogue, accounts, saved bags, and orders are kept with Supabase, the service the boutique uses. The page carries a publishable key. The house’s notes on that key say it is meant for the browser, and that the tables are limited so the key can read the active catalogue and act on the signed-in client’s own rows. A service-role key is not published on this site.

Typefaces are requested from Google Fonts. The sign-in library is loaded from jsDelivr. Stripe is used only when payments are turned on, and then on Stripe’s page. Instagram is a link. The site does not embed a tracker from it.

A private back office lets people the house has marked as administrators see orders, the catalogue, and client records. It is not a public page.

Maison news

News is sent only if you ask, at checkout or in the account. You may clear the choice in the account, or write to cittacico@gmail.com and ask the house to stop.

What this note does not claim

The house does not say that information is never passed on. The services named above receive what the page sends them to do the work described. The house has not published how long it keeps an order or an account.

The account page lets you correct your details, replace a saved address, change the password, and sign out. There is no control on the site that deletes an account. To ask for a correction the page cannot make, or to ask that an account be closed, write to cittacico@gmail.com.

The house does not address its accounts or its boutique to children.

If the house changes what the site does, it will change this page. The date above is the one to read. The terms of use and the cookie notice sit with it.
