Terms of use

The understanding for reading the house, opening an account, and ordering from the boutique.

Last updated November 2026

These terms describe the Cittàcico website as it stands: the house, and the online boutique built into it.

The house has not published a registered company name, a tax number, or a court. It presents itself as a New York maison. Questions go to cittacico@gmail.com.

Using the site

You may read the house, write to it, and, when the boutique is open, keep an account and place an order. On the public site the boutique is closed to visitors until the house opens it. Shop pages then return you to the house. These terms still describe the boutique, because the pages exist and will take an order when they are open.

Do not interfere with the site, attempt the back office without being invited, or present yourself as the house.

Accounts

An account needs an email and a password of at least eight characters. You may add your name, a date of birth, a sex or leave it unsaid, a country and region, a telephone, a delivery address, and a choice about house correspondence. Confirmation of the account, and a reset of the password, are sent by email. You are responsible for that email and that password.

Signing in keeps the bag and the order history with the account. Signing out clears the bag on that device. A guest may check out with an email alone. The checkout states that a guest order is recorded against that email and appears if you later register with the same address.

The boutique and an order

Pieces, prices, and availability are shown as the house keeps them, in US dollars. A bag may be kept in the browser and, once you are signed in, with the account.

Placing an order sends the selection, the email, and the delivery address to the house. It is a request the house records. While payment is not taken on this site, it is not, by itself, a completed sale.

Payment

No payment is taken at checkout while the house has no merchant account. The payment step says that the house will write to confirm the order and arrange settlement. Nothing is charged on this page.

When the house enables payments, that step continues to Stripe. Card, Apple Pay, and other wallets Stripe offers are taken on Stripe’s page, not on this site. If you leave before paying, the bag remains. The bag is cleared once the order is paid or, while payments are off, once the order is recorded and the confirmation page opens. If a payment is declined, the failure page states that no charge has been made.

Delivery, tax, and returns

Checkout shows shipping at no charge and tax at zero. It also says that complimentary standard delivery applies when fulfillment is live, and that tax will be calculated from the delivery address. The figures to rely on are those the house confirms in writing. The house has not published the countries it will ship to, a delivery time, or a duty.

The checkout mentions complimentary returns within the house’s policy. A numbered window is not published here. Client services answers questions of sizing, lead time, shipping, and exchange. Write to cittacico@gmail.com before you send a piece back. Appointments, care, and orders are also described on Client services.

Visits

The Locations page lists where the house receives clients, including a New York studio by appointment and a European studio in Milano whose street address is not yet announced. A visit is confirmed only when the house says so.

The house’s work

The name Cittàcico, the campaigns, the photographs, and the text on this site belong to the house. Please do not copy them for your own trade. The principal designer is credited on the site as Universe Chen.

The limits of these pages

The site may be unavailable, and a piece may leave the catalogue. The house does not promise that every order can be fulfilled. While payment is not taken here, an order is settled only when the house confirms it.

These terms do not name a registered company and do not choose a court. If something in an order needs to be put right, begin with client services.

The house may revise these terms as the boutique opens and as payment is connected. The date on this page is the one to read. See also the privacy policy and the cookie notice.
